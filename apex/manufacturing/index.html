AT THE CORE.

Apex is American owned and operated in New York. Its dedicated manufacturing facility in Sri Lanka brings locally sourced raw materials and puck engineering together.

The standard APEX and markless APEX PRO ranges give players and organizations a clear choice for their needs. The work behind both is about the same things: the compound, the edge and consistent performance.

Apex’s history includes the acquisition of American Hockey Puck Company and investment in its Sri Lankan facility.

MATERIAL SELECTION

Apex uses raw materials sourced in Sri Lanka. Its formulations balance the hardness and resilience required for puck performance.

02

DEDICATED MANUFACTURING

Production takes place in a facility focused exclusively on hockey pucks, with a consistent product and process at the center of the operation.

03

THE KNURLED EDGE

Tight edge knurling is a defining part of the Apex design. It gives the stick a textured contact surface for puck handling.
