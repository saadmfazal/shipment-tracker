HOW PUCKQUEST WORKS

OPEN. GUESS.
LOCK IT.

The physical puck can change hands only while its code is unregistered. Once claimed, it is permanently locked to that collector account.

01 / PICK YOUR PACK

Choose the odds

Buy League, Conference or Division. Each sealed pack contains two physical mini pucks.

02 / REVEAL CLUE

Read the artwork

Open the packet and inspect the printed theme. The art is the clue—not the answer.

03 / GUESS & REGISTER

Commit the code

Enter the puck code and initial team guess within 15 days of original purchase.

04 / COLLECT

Build the board

Guesses stay editable until a prize claim. Registered pucks remain permanently in the original locker.

Important: unregistered pucks may be traded, gifted or sold before registration. Registered pucks cannot be transferred between accounts.

CLAIM CONTROL

ONE RESULT.
PASS OR FAIL.

No individual correctness feedback is shown. A first failed full-collection claim starts one seven-day correction window; later retries do not restart it.

01 / PURCHASE WINDOW

Register within 15 days

Code and initial guess are submitted within 15 days of the original purchase date.

02 / BEFORE PASS

Evidence review

Purchased-puck claims require a clear selfie with the pucks, approved purchase evidence, a public #PuckQuestWin post, and identity, eligibility, tax and payment setup.

03 / RESULT

Overall answer only

PASS initiates final approval and payment. FAIL does not identify which guesses are incorrect.

04 / CORRECTION

Seven days, once

The first failed full-collection attempt starts one correction window. Retries do not reset the deadline.
