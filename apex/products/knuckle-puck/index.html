UPDATED GAME RULES

SET. FLICK.
SCORE.

01 / What you need

Two players, one mini-puck, and a smooth, level table.

02 / Set up your goal

The defending player rests the index finger and pinky of the same hand on the tabletop, pointing toward the opposing player and spaced apart to form the goal. Keep the opening clear and both goalposts still. Agree on a goal width and shooting distance for the game.

03 / How to play

The shooter gets up to three attempts to flick the mini-puck through the opening. Every shot must be a finger flick: no pushing, dragging, or sliding the puck by hand. Return the puck to the agreed starting line before each attempt. A goal counts when the entire puck enters the opening. Once a goal is scored, it still counts if the puck bounces back out, bounces over the hand, or goes off the table. The first successful goal ends the turn.

04 / Scoring

First attempt: 3 points. Second attempt: 2 points. Third attempt: 1 point. Three misses: 0 points. Only one goal may be scored per turn.

05 / How to win

Once either player reaches 10 or more points, finish the round so both players have taken an equal number of turns. The higher score wins. Ties continue with complete additional turns until one player leads after both have shot.

06 / Off-the-table penalty

If a shot leaves the table without first scoring, the opposing player receives one penalty shot. The responsible player places both fists on the tabletop, knuckles down. The opponent places the puck a few inches from the offending player’s knuckles and takes one controlled flick toward them. The penalty shot awards no points. Whether unused regular attempts resume afterward remains pending Apex’s final rule confirmation.
